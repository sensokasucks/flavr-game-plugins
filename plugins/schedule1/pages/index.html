Chatters who type !customer get their name on a customer in your Schedule I game. Chat hears when they text an order and when the deal goes through. Hidden and red-flagged chatters never get a name. !customer leave takes someone off; you can do it here too.

These apply straight away, no restart. The mod address and token are on Settings → Plugins, next to Enabled.
